HDR Capstone Project - Github

Project to look at various methods of maintaining detail throughout an image with higher dynamic range than a camera can handle. This project is written in C++ both for my own practice, and I also wanted to do all of the image handling on my own instead of using packages. I thought the speed of C++ would be nice.

Objective

Dynamic range is the measurement between the darkest and brightest spot in a scence. The sensor on most cameras has a limited dynamic range. This is for many reasons, but mostly for color differentiation. Although helful, this physical limitation means that detail is often lost in scenes with a high dynamic range. Our eyes can quickly adjust for the brightness of an object, so it feels like our eyes can see detail both in a dark room and out a bright window. In this project, I explore a few methods of maintaining detail in an entire image despite a high dynaic range scene. Maintaining detail comes at a cost, often losing color accuracy.
