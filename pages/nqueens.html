NQueens - Github

Coding problem to count the number of queens needed for an NxN board to cover the board. The goal was to come up with the fastest method of counting.

Methodology

I chose to try and remove the board, conceptually, to help speed up the process of placing and removing queens. Mixed success.

Results

Although I was somewhat happy with my solution, it is still a little slower than I would like. I think there must be a better way to represent the board to reduce the amount of trial and error that comes with placing and removing each queen.
